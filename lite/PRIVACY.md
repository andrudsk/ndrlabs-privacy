# Privacy Policy: NDR-One Lite

**Effective date:** 2026-10-03  
**App:** NDR-One Lite, a watch face for Garmin Connect IQ devices  
**Publisher:** NDRLabs  
**Contact:** ndrlabs.co@gmail.com

## Summary

NDR-One Lite is a watch face that runs only on your Garmin watch. It does not collect personal data and it sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else: it requests neither the Communications nor the Background permission, and it makes no network requests. The data it reads to draw the face stays on the watch. This edition does not request the Positioning permission and never reads your location.

## Permissions and why they are requested

## Other data shown on the face

Like other watch faces, NDR-One Lite also shows data that Connect IQ provides without a separate permission, such as steps, floors, calories, the current heart rate, the battery level, the number of unread notifications (the count only, never their content) and the weather that Garmin's own apps deliver to the watch. This data is read on the watch and is not sent anywhere by the face.

## What is stored on the watch

The face keeps its settings and a small amount of working data in its own storage on the watch, such as hourly summaries for the heart rate chart covering the last day (the highest and lowest heart rate of each hour). It never stores your location. This data stays on the watch and is not sent anywhere by the face.

## Settings

NDR-One Lite has no phone settings. Its few options (which elements are shown, seconds and AM/PM) are changed on the watch and stored there. They contain no personal data.

## Crash reports

If the face stops with an error, Garmin may collect an error report and make it available to the developer through Garmin's Connect IQ developer tools. The reports the developer receives contain the type of error and where in the face's code it happened, the face version, the watch model and its firmware version, the watch's language setting, and how often and when the error occurred. The developer does not receive your name, your Garmin account, your location or your health data from these reports. The face contains no crash reporting or analytics code of its own. Garmin's collection of these reports is covered by Garmin's privacy policy.

## What the face does not do

- It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
- It makes no network requests and runs no background tasks.
- It does not read the content of your notifications, messages, contacts or calendar entries.
- It does not sell or share any data, because it collects none.

## Third-party services

None. NDR-One Lite uses only Garmin's own Connect IQ APIs on the watch. Weather and complications are provided by Garmin.

## Children

NDR-One Lite is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age.

## Changes to this policy

If the way NDR-One Lite handles data changes, this page will be revised and its effective date changed before the new version is published.
