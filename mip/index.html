Privacy Policy: NDR-One MIP

Effective date: 2026-10-03
App: NDR-One MIP, a watch face for Garmin Connect IQ devices
Publisher: NDRLabs
Contact: ndrlabs.co@gmail.com

Summary

NDR-One MIP is a watch face that runs only on your Garmin watch. It does not collect personal data and it sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else: it requests neither the Communications nor the Background permission, and it makes no network requests. The data it reads to draw the face stays on the watch.

Permissions and why they are requested

The Connect IQ Store lists the permissions below. Each one is used only to draw the watch face on your watch.

PermissionWhy it is requested
ComplicationSubscriberReads Garmin's first-party complications so the face can show metrics such as training status, recovery time, Body Battery, Pulse Ox, respiration rate, sleep score, calories and weekly running and cycling distance. On watches that support it, a long press on a metric uses Garmin's own API to open that metric's glance. These values originate on your watch and are not sent anywhere by this app.
PositioningUsed only to work out the time of the next sunrise or sunset. The face first uses the location that Garmin's weather service reports for its latest observation. If there is none, it reads the last position your watch already has (Position.getInfo). The app never calls Position.enableLocationEvents, so it never turns on location tracking. The position is kept in memory only while the face runs, is never written to storage and is never transmitted by this app.
SensorHistoryReads only the most recent heart rate, stress and Body Battery sample your watch has already stored, to show current values and to add each hour to the chart as it passes. This edition never reads back a longer history. Nothing is streamed and nothing is uploaded.
UserProfileReads, on the watch only: your heart rate zones and maximum heart rate (or your birth year, to estimate it when it is not set), to colour heart rate by zone; your resting heart rate, to show it; your VO2 max (running, or cycling when there is no running value) with your birth year and sex, to place it in its fitness band; and your weight, to show it and, with your height, birth year and sex, to estimate resting calories for the calories ring. Read-only. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Other data shown on the face

Like other watch faces, NDR-One MIP also shows data that Connect IQ provides without a separate permission, such as steps, floors, calories, the current heart rate, the battery level, the number of unread notifications (the count only, never their content) and the weather that Garmin's own apps deliver to the watch. This data is read on the watch and is not sent anywhere by the face.

What is stored on the watch

The face keeps its settings and a small amount of working data in its own storage on the watch: hourly summaries for the chart covering the last day (for example the highest and lowest heart rate of each hour) and hourly step counts. It never stores your location. This data stays on the watch and is not sent anywhere by the face.

Settings

You can change the face's settings on the watch, or from your phone in the Connect IQ app (also reachable from Garmin Connect). The settings are display choices only, such as the theme, the layout, which metric sits in each place and similar options, and they contain no personal data. Settings changed on the phone are carried between your phone and your watch by Garmin's own apps and services; that transfer is covered by Garmin's privacy policy, not by this one. The face itself does not send its settings anywhere.

Crash reports

If the face stops with an error, Garmin may collect an error report and make it available to the developer through Garmin's Connect IQ developer tools. The reports the developer receives contain the type of error and where in the face's code it happened, the face version, the watch model and its firmware version, the watch's language setting, and how often and when the error occurred. The developer does not receive your name, your Garmin account, your location or your health data from these reports. The face contains no crash reporting or analytics code of its own. Garmin's collection of these reports is covered by Garmin's privacy policy.

What the face does not do

  • It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • It makes no network requests and runs no background tasks.
  • It does not read the content of your notifications, messages, contacts or calendar entries.
  • It does not sell or share any data, because it collects none.

Third-party services

None. NDR-One MIP uses only Garmin's own Connect IQ APIs on the watch. Weather, complications and the transfer of settings from the phone are provided by Garmin.

Children

NDR-One MIP is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the way NDR-One MIP handles data changes, this page will be revised and its effective date changed before the new version is published.
